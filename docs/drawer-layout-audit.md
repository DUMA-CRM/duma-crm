# Drawer layout audit

Reviewed on 18 October 2026. Latest consistency pass: all modal drawer render points now supply a meaningful header icon or existing avatar/status mark; all collapsible FormSection cards supply an icon. Header guidance has moved into the body, and redundant introductions have been removed. Disclosure cards keep a steady background on hover. This is a source inventory and layout decision record, not a claim that every data-dependent drawer was opened in the browser.

76 render points across 72 distinct owning components, including wrappers, loading/error branches and the non-modal Ask DUMA panel. Short workflows keep their primary controls visible; longer forms progressively disclose secondary fields. Orders and Edit customer remain the visual references. Plain section headings use text; transfer Items and Note are collapsible icon cards matching customer Preferences. Drawer field labels share required/optional wording across input, date, time and custom field controls. Action, status and individual field-row icons remain purposeful. Remaining section titles use Orders’ direct h3 typography and 8px spacing. Redundant headings have been removed where the drawer title and field labels already provide context (transfer route, modifier/category/group details, counted quantities, and container cost/split/combine fields).
